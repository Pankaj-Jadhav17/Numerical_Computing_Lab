===== ASSIGNMENT QUESTIONS =====
Q1. Does Richardson always give a smaller error than Central Difference?
Richardson usually gives a smaller truncation error, but it may not always be better when h becomes very small because round-off error can dominate.

Q2. What happens when h is reduced by 10?
For Central Difference, the theoretical error decreases by about 100 times. For Richardson, the theoretical error decreases by about 10000 times.

Q3. Does Richardson continue to improve for very small h?
No. Floating-point round-off error eventually becomes important and the total error can increase.

Q4. Does the log-log slope support the theory?
The slope should be close to 2 for Central Difference and close to 4 for Richardson before round-off error becomes dominant.

Q5. Does Richardson achieve O(h^4)?
Yes, when truncation error is the main source of error. Richardson removes the leading O(h^2) term.

Q6. What is the role of floating-point round-off error?
When h becomes very small, subtracting nearly equal floating-point values loses accuracy. Dividing by a small h increases the effect of this error.
