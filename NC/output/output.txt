===== ANSWERS TO ASSIGNMENT QUESTIONS =====
Q1. Does Richardson always give a smaller error than Central Difference?
    Mostly, but NOT always: at the smallest step sizes, round-off error in computing D(h) and D(h/2) and combining them can make R(h) worse than D(h).

Q2. By approximately what factor does the error decrease when h is reduced by 10?
    D(h): ~94.8063x per decade (matches O(h^2): 10^2 = 100)
    R(h): ~1782.5130x per decade (matches O(h^4): 10^4 = 10000)

Q3. Does Richardson continue to improve as h becomes very small?
    No. Once round-off dominates, R(h) can stop decreasing and increase again.
